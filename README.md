## About DREMTIA Packaging

**DREMTIA** ([dremtia.com](https://dremtia.com/)) is a B2B packaging development and manufacturing partner for skincare, beauty, fragrance and personal care brands worldwide. We combine **in-house glass and plastic packaging production** (supported by 400 private moulds and existing tooling) with a **long-term partner paper-box factory**, enabling brands to specify, decorate and ship their complete packaging system — primary container, dispensing closure, surface decoration and secondary carton — through one coordinated team.
