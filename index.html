1. Material Hubs & Core Packaging Categories

Glass Packaging (In-House Factory)

Type III soda-lime and Type I borosilicate glass bottles and jars with matched pipettes, treatment pumps, sprayers and roller balls.

  • Glass Dropper Bottles (5ml–100ml)
  • Glass Serum Bottles (10ml–50ml)
  • Glass Cream Jars (15ml–100ml & 330g)
  • Glass Lotion Pump Bottles
  • Glass Essential Oil Dropper Bottles
  • Glass Roll-On Bottles (5ml–15ml)
  • Glass Perfume Spray Bottles & Flacons
  • Amber Glass Bottles · Frosted Glass · Custom Glass Moulds

Plastic & Airless Packaging (In-House Factory)

Shatter-resistant PET, PETG, PP, HDPE, Acrylic, PE/ABS and documented PCR containers engineered for active protection and DTC parcels.

  • Plastic Airless Pump Bottles (Piston & Pouch)
  • Plastic Airless Press-Pump Jars
  • Plastic Serum Bottles & Plastic Lotion Bottles
  • Plastic Cosmetic Bottles (30ml–500ml)
  • Plastic Cream Jars & PP Cosmetic Jars
  • PET Cosmetic Bottles & HDPE Squeeze Bottles
  • Cosmetic Squeeze Tubes
  • PCR Plastic Packaging & Refillable Systems

2. Formula-to-Packaging Engineering Matrix

Application Recommended Format Critical Specification Check Solution Guide
Serums & Actives 15ml–50ml Glass Dropper or Airless Bottle Airless piston for retinol/vitamin C; amber glass for UV protection Serum Packaging
Moisturisers & Balms 30g–100g Glass Jar or Airless Press-Pump Jar Viscosity vs. piston lift; PP inner disc or induction seal liner Moisturizer Packaging
Cleansers & Washes 100ml–250ml Foaming Pump, HDPE Squeeze or Tube Wet-hand grip; BOPP laminated label resistance to humidity Cleanser Packaging
Lotions & Emulsions 50ml–500ml Glass or Plastic Lotion Pump Bottle Pump output (0.3ml–1.0ml) matched to viscosity; lockable head Lotion Packaging
Facial & Essential Oils 5ml–50ml Amber/Frosted Dropper or Orifice Reducer Butyl or high-grade silicone bulb (NBR swells in essential oils) Facial Oil · Essential Oil
Sunscreen & SPF 30ml–100ml Squeeze Tube or Airless Pump Bottle UV filter compatibility soak test on gaskets, liners and inks Sunscreen Packaging
Eye Creams & Roll-Ons 5ml–20ml Steel-Ball Roll-On or Mini Airless Pot 8.5mm/11mm roller housing tolerance; cooling metal applicator Eye Cream Packaging
Fragrance & Perfume 15ml–100ml Glass Flacon (FEA 15 Crimp / Screw) Alcohol-compatible gasket & dip tube; fitted carton insert Fragrance Packaging
